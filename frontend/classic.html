About GAUSS-O-MATIC 3000

Fits are computed server-side by CERN ROOT (Minuit) and drawn by JSROOT. Nothing you enter is stored on the server — use Save document to keep your work.

Built by Minh Tran (2026) as a redesign of Dr. D. Beznosko's chi2 tool (Clayton State University), without accounts or server-side storage.
