Uncertainties & Analyze Data

Measurement uncertainties enter the fit as weights: a point with a smaller uncertainty pulls the curve more strongly. With uncertainties on both axes, ROOT combines them by the effective-variance method - each point's effective variance is σy² + (f′(x)·σx)², re-evaluated as the parameters change. With no Y uncertainties the fit is unweighted and χ² is reported in arbitrary units.

Analyze Data

The Analyze Data page calculates further quantities from your measurements and fitted parameters and propagates their uncertainties automatically. Define a new column or single value with an expression (a difference, a ratio, or a derived physical quantity), and the result carries the correctly combined uncertainty. Columns can track units, so a computed quantity reports both its value and its unit. Results and calculated columns carry through to the export.
