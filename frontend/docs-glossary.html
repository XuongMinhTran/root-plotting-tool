Glossary

Model
A function f(x; p) proposed to describe the data, with parameters p to be determined.
Parameter
A quantity the fit adjusts, written [0], [1], … in a ROOT expression or as a symbol in the equation editor.
Starting value (initial guess)
The value of a parameter at which the minimiser begins. Not an uncertainty, and not a constraint.
Standard uncertainty
One standard deviation of a measured or derived quantity: the half-width of its 68% interval. The only kind of uncertainty the tool uses.
Residual
Data minus model at a point, yi − f(xi).
Pull
A residual divided by the point's effective uncertainty. For a correct model and correct uncertainties the pulls follow a standard normal distribution.
χ² (chi-square)
The sum over fitted points of pull². The quantity the fit minimises.
NDF
Degrees of freedom: fitted points minus free parameters.
χ²/NDF (reduced chi-square)
χ² per degree of freedom; about 1 when the model and the uncertainties are both right. Its spread is roughly √(2/NDF).
p-value
The probability that a correct model would produce a χ² at least as large as the one observed.
Poisson deviance
The likelihood-ratio statistic reported for histogram fits by Poisson likelihood; plays the role of χ² and approaches it at large counts.
Minuit
ROOT's minimiser. Its status code is the convergence status in the report.
Hesse matrix
The matrix of second derivatives of χ² at the minimum; its inverse is the covariance matrix.
Covariance matrix
The matrix whose diagonal holds the squared parameter uncertainties and whose off-diagonal elements express how pairs of parameters vary together.
Correlation
Covariance normalised to the range −1 to 1. Near ±1 means the data determine only a combination of the two parameters.
Effective variance
σy² + (f′·σx)²: the method by which an x uncertainty is included in the fit.
Confidence band
The uncertainty of the fitted curve itself at each x, from the parameter covariance. Not a prediction interval for new data.
Fit range
The interval of x inside which points are fitted; points outside are plotted but ignored.
Point exclusion
A single measurement left out of the fit, with a recorded reason, while remaining in the data and on the plot.
Stale
A fit result whose data or model have changed since it was computed. Calculations that depend on it are blocked until it is refitted.
Propagation of uncertainty
Carrying the uncertainties and covariances of inputs through a calculation to the uncertainty of its result; done here to first order with numerical derivatives.
Session
Everything in both pages: datasets, models, results and calculations. Autosaved in the browser; saved to a .json file with Save.
