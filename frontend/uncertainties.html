Typing, units and uncertainty
  • Type a symbol's name to use a source: x, or \tau then Space for τ. A symbol that matches no source asks what it is.
  • / makes a fraction, ^ a power, _ a subscript, \sqrt a square root. Functions need parentheses: ln(x), exp(x), sin(x). Arrow keys leave a fraction or exponent.
  • Units come from the sources. A source without a unit counts as a plain number; give it one under the formula if it has one. The result unit is suggested; type another compatible unit (e.g. nm, mm/s, J/(mol*K)) to convert.
  • Uncertainties propagate to first order with numerical derivatives, keeping fit covariance and shared inputs. Separate sources are independent. Rows pair up by position; a single value applies to every row. This can be inaccurate near singularities or with large, strongly nonlinear uncertainties.
