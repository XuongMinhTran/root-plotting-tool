The function

Pick a shape below, or write your own. x is the measurement, and [0], [1], … are the numbers ROOT will find for you.

The function gives counts per unit X; its integral across a bin predicts that bin's count. Poisson likelihood keeps empty bins and suits sparse data; χ² leaves empty bins out. The fit range picks bins by their centres.

About GAUSS-O-MATIC 3000

Fits are computed by CERN ROOT (Minuit) and drawn by JSROOT. Nothing you enter is stored on the server — use Save to keep your work as a file.

Built by Minh Tran (2026) as a redesign of Dr. D. Beznosko's chi2 tool (Clayton State University), without accounts or server-side storage.
